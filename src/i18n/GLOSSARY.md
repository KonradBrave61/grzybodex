# Grzybodex — English glossary (one term = one translation in the whole app)

Polish is the source language. English must keep the **meaning 1:1**, especially every safety text
(poisoning, deadly species, protected species, "this is not a mycologist's opinion"). Never soften a warning.
Plain, short British/International English, sentence case, no Pokémon/Pokédex references.
